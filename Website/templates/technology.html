Our Technology

Advanced systems working together for safer airports

Transforming Airport Navigation

The Skyeye-1 system combines multiple advanced technologies to create a comprehensive solution for low-visibility airport operations.

Central Control System

The heart of our technology is a sophisticated central control system positioned at the top of the air traffic control tower. This system utilizes advanced radar technology to track all aircraft in the vicinity of the airport.

The control system constantly monitors aircraft positions and orchestrates the deployment and movement of our drone fleet, ensuring optimal positioning for guidance and emergency response.

Key features include:

  • Real-time aircraft tracking within a 100-mile radius
  • Automated drone deployment algorithms
  • Predictive weather integration
  • Redundant systems for 99.999% uptime
  • Seamless integration with existing ATC software

ADS-B Transponder Integration

Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) technology forms the backbone of our aircraft tracking system. Each Skyeye-1 drone is equipped with dual ADS-B receivers that can detect and interpret signals from aircraft transponders.

This technology allows our drones to precisely locate aircraft in 3D space, even in conditions where visual identification is impossible. The system continuously updates position data at a rate of 10Hz, ensuring highly responsive guidance.

Benefits of our ADS-B implementation include:

  • Precise aircraft positioning with centimeter-level accuracy
  • Automatic identification of aircraft type and flight parameters
  • Seamless operation with both 1090 MHz and 978 MHz frequencies
  • Compliance with global aviation standards

High-Intensity Beacon System

The Skyeye-1's most visible technology is its proprietary high-intensity beacon light system. This powerful red light is specifically designed to penetrate through fog, rain, snow, and other low-visibility conditions.

The beacon employs advanced LED technology with specialized optics that create a light pattern optimized for pilot visibility. The light is calibrated to be highly visible without causing discomfort or distraction to pilots.

Technical specifications include:

  • 360° visibility with focused forward projection
  • Penetration of up to 5 miles in heavy fog conditions
  • Pulsing patterns for enhanced visibility
  • Automatic intensity adjustment based on environmental conditions
  • Low power consumption for extended operation time

Emergency Response Technology

Our emergency response variants of the Skyeye-1 incorporate specialized technology for rapid incident control. These drones feature enhanced batteries, more powerful rotors for increased speed, and a sophisticated fire suppressant delivery system.

The emergency response system includes thermal imaging cameras that can identify hotspots and assess the nature of an incident before human responders arrive. This data is transmitted in real-time to emergency services.

Key technology components include:

  • Compact yet highly effective fire suppressant delivery system
  • High-definition thermal imaging with automated incident assessment
  • Enhanced communication systems with direct emergency services integration
  • Rapid deployment capabilities with <30 second response time
  • Extended flight time for sustained emergency operations

Technology Integration

How our systems work together seamlessly

System Integration Workflow

  1. The Central Control System monitors all aircraft via radar and ADS-B data.
  2. When an aircraft approaching in low visibility is detected, the system deploys a Skyeye-1 Guidance Drone.
  3. Using ADS-B transponder data, the drone positions itself at a safe distance in front of the aircraft.
  4. The high-intensity beacon provides visual guidance to pilots, leading them safely to their gate.
  5. The Human Supervision Team monitors the entire operation and can intervene if necessary.
  6. In case of emergency, Emergency Response Drones are deployed automatically while alerting human responders.

This integrated approach ensures seamless operations even in the most challenging visibility conditions.
