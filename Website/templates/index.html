Drone Traffic Management

Our autonomous air traffic drones perform the same roles as humans but more efficiently. These drones significantly increase the safety of airport ground traffic in low visibility operations, when pilots have the most trouble navigating taxiways, especially at larger airports.

A large central control unit on top of the air traffic control tower relays aircraft positions via radar. Using ADS-B transponders, our drones locate and move to specific aircraft around the airport. With powerful beacon lights, they hover safely in front of designated aircraft to guide them to their gates, reducing incidents due to poor visibility.

Emergency Response System

Our emergency response variants, equipped with higher-capacity batteries, more powerful rotors, and nozzles to spray fire-suppressant foam, remain on standby 24/7 throughout the airport.

These drones deploy instantly when needed, acting as first responders to begin controlling emergency situations, while simultaneously notifying air traffic control supervisors and emergency response vehicles. Human intervention remains our failsafe, with supervisors on duty to ensure there are no issues with the drones themselves.
