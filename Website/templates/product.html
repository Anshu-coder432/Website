Meet Skyeye-1

Our flagship drone system designed specifically for airport navigation in challenging visibility conditions.

The Skyeye-1 drone features a distinctive bright red guidance light that provides clear visual cues to pilots during fog, rain, and other low-visibility scenarios. Working in conjunction with airport control systems, Skyeye-1 dramatically reduces the risk of incidents while maintaining efficient traffic flow.

Highly visible beacon light system
Integrated ADS-B transponder technology
Precision hover capabilities with redundant systems
Weather-resistant design for all-condition operation

Technical Specifications

Engineered for performance, reliability and safety

Physical Specifications

  • Dimensions: 24" x 24" x 12" (60cm x 60cm x 30cm)

Tracking Technology
