About AirGuide Technologies

Our Story

From vision to revolutionizing airport safety

A Mission Born from Experience

AirGuide Technologies was founded in 2025 by a team of aviation professionals who had witnessed firsthand the challenges and risks associated with low-visibility operations at busy airports.

After a near-miss incident during heavy fog at a major international airport, our founders recognized the need for a more reliable visual guidance system that could supplement traditional radio communications and radar guidance.

Drawing on decades of combined experience in aviation, air traffic control, and drone technology, they developed the concept for what would eventually become the Skyeye-1 system—a revolutionary approach to airport navigation safety.

Innovation Through Collaboration

The development of Skyeye-1 was a collaborative effort involving aviation authorities, airport operators, pilots, and air traffic controllers from around the world.

Through extensive consultation and testing, we refined our technology to address the real-world challenges faced by aviation professionals. This collaborative approach has been central to our success, ensuring that our solutions are not just technically innovative, but practically valuable in operational environments.

Today, we continue to work closely with the aviation community, constantly refining and enhancing our technology based on feedback and changing needs.
