Drone Traffic Management

We will create an autonomous air traffic drone that can perform the same roles as humans but more efficiently. These drones will be able to significantly increase the safety of airport ground traffic in low visibility operations, as this is when pilots have the most trouble navigating taxiways, especially at larger airports.

A large central piece will be on top of the air traffic control tower, relaying aircraft positions in the airspace via radar. Using Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) transponders, the drones will be able to locate and move to specific aircraft around the airport. Using powerful beacon/fog lights, the drones will hover safely in front of the designated aircraft and guide it to the gate, lowering the chance of an incident due to visibility impairment. Emergency response variants of the drone, equipped with higher-capacity batteries, more powerful rotors, and nozzles to spray fire-suppressant foam, will be on standby 24/7 all over the airport. These drones will be deployed when needed, acting as first responders to start controlling the situation, while also notifying air traffic control supervisors and emergency response vehicles. Human intervention is the failsafe. Several supervisors will be on duty, whose responsibility is to ensure there are no issues with the drones themselves. If there is any problem, the aircraft in the area will be instructed to hold in place until the problem is fixed.



AI Controlled Drones

AI-powered drones are advanced unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) that are equipped with artificial intelligence (AI) technology.
